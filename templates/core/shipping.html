1. Our Delivery Options

We aim to get your parts and items to you safely and simply. The available methods, fees and lead times are shown live at checkout, but the general options are:

2. Standard Delivery

Courier delivery to your door anywhere in South Africa. A flat standard fee applies per order and is calculated by us — always at checkout, never added later. Orders usually reach you within a short, predictable window that we confirm with your order confirmation.

3. Free Delivery on Small Items

When your order total reaches a minimum amount, standard delivery is free. This is applied automatically — you do not need to choose a special “free delivery” option. Checkout shows the minimum, tells you how much more you need to get closer, and once you qualify the standard fee is struck through and shown as R0.00, so you always see exactly what you’ll pay before you confirm.

This offer covers small items. Large item delivery is charged at its own fee and is never waived by the minimum, however high your order total is — moving something bulky costs us more than the standard courier run. If your cart contains a large item, checkout keeps that fee visible and does not tell you to spend more to qualify.

4. Local Pickup

Prefer to collect? Choose Local Pickup at checkout for a R0.00 fee. We’ll WhatsApp you the moment your order is ready, with our address: {{ cfg.address }}. Please contact us via WhatsApp or phone before you visit so we can have everything packed and waiting for you.

5. How Delivery Fees Are Calculated

  • The fee is calculated on our side at checkout, based on the delivery method you select, your order total, and how each item in your cart is delivered.
  • Each product carries its own delivery type: standard uses our flat fee, free costs nothing, custom has its own set fee, and large item is charged at the large item rate.
  • Buying more of the same kind of item does not multiply the fee. A standard fee and a large item fee are each charged once per order, not per product.
  • It is added to your order total in one place — the order summary — before you pay.
  • The amount you see at checkout is the amount you pay. We never add delivery charges to your order after it is confirmed.

6. Delivery Times

Delivery windows appear at checkout and with your confirmation. They depend on your location and our workshop schedule HOLIDAYS and unforeseen delays excluded. If we’re ever running late we’ll let you know on WhatsApp as soon as we do.

7. Failed Delivery / No-One Home

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, we’ll contact you on WhatsApp to rearrange. Reasonable redelivery may be arranged; repeated failed attempts may eventually be treated as Local Pickup so your order isn’t stuck in a courier depot.

8. Damaged or Missing Items

Receipt of an item is subject to our returns process — if anything arrives damaged or is missing, please tell us within 7 days (see our Refund & Returns Policy).
